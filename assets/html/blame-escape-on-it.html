Part IV · The salience corpus principle
"YouTube transcriptions are post-hoc symbolic representations — someone watched the video, felt the ♭2 → 1 pull, then typed chords into a tab website. The emotional salience got stripped in translation. What remains is notation without groove-weight." — This conversation, on why Gemini's data moat failed

The corpus taxonomy is now clean:

X / Twitter
Salience corpus. Affect-weighted signal. What hits people, argued in real time. Layer 02 detector — h(t) spikes are social and public. People only post when something hits them hard enough to type.
YouTube
Volume corpus. High coverage, low salience density. Good for pattern frequency, terrible for modal gravity. Gemini's moat is a liability when the signal requires emotional weighting.
GitHub / Docs
Structure corpus. Useful for architecture, useless for groove. The shape of the system without the weight of experience.
Scholar / PubMed
Formal corpus. Post-hoc symbolic representation, same salience-stripping problem as tabs. Ministry reports live here.
Architectural principle for Ukubona: Cross-validate every corpus against its affect-weighted equivalent. When building the Individual twin, clinical records are the YouTube problem — high volume, salience-stripped. Patient forums and caregiver Twitter threads are the X solution. When building the Government twin, ministry reports are YouTube. Community health workers arguing on WhatsApp groups are X. Where they diverge, the phantom lives.
