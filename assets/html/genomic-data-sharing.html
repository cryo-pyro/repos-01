01 · L₀ · General Systems Theory

The A Priori State Space

Sensing layer · upstream boundary conditions · prior before any ethical parameter is set
L_tot = L_0 + Σ w_i·L_i + ε

L₀ is exogenous. General Systems Theory establishes the prior — the structural facts of the world before any ethical principle is applied or optimised. Its core components are interdependence, feedback loops, system boundaries, and dynamic interactions: the four elements that define the state space within which all downstream ethical decisions must operate.

GST maps across four simultaneous levels — individual, institutional, national, and international — explicitly incorporating platforms such as H3Africa Bionet, EGA, and dbGaP. No Ugandan or African researcher operates in isolation from these global boundary conditions. Together they constitute L₀: the a priori parameter space that every downstream weight w_i inherits.

Individual Factors (gender, age, values, genetic & molecular properties — independent of family structure) and Practices (platforms, policies, guidelines) are the two parallel input vectors of L₀. They converge on the Stakeholder network — system nodes including lawyers, regulators, RECs, funders, CAB members, publishers, researchers, and participants — all operating inside the hard boundary of institutional, national, and international law.

L₀ fully constrains what Principlism can achieve. Ethical possibility is a subset of the state space L₀ defines. No w_i, however large, can exceed L₀.

02 · Σw_iL_i · Principlism

The Attention-Weighted Constraint Layer

Mediating filter · w_i = softmax(Q_i K_j / √d) · attention is all you need
L_tot = L_0 + Σ w_i · L_i + ε

Principlism (Beauchamp & Childress) is the Σw_iL_i layer — a set of attention-weighted ethical constraints on permissible action within the state space L₀ has already defined. Autonomy, Beneficence, Non-maleficence, and Justice are not aspirational ideals here. They are loss terms L_i, each weighted by w_i, where the weights are determined by the cultural salience, historical context, and empirical urgency of each layer.

The attention weights matter critically. A household land-sale story — a concrete social shame event — can and should dominate w_i for the Social & Relational Factors node, far outweighing an abstract policy memo. Social & Relational Factors (trust, collaboration, communication, but also stigmatization, discrimination, and social shame) are endogenous variables in the loss function, not external risks to be footnoted. Ignoring them sets those w_i to zero — which produces frameworks that are ethically aspirational but empirically inert in African contexts.

Community Engagement operationalises autonomy (w_autonomy maximised) through meaningful informed consent while mitigating social risk. The Ethical Concerns filter — informed consent, re-identifiability risk, data misuse, compromise — is where Σw_iL_i is evaluated: only vectors that satisfy all weighted constraints propagate to the ε layer.

Principles do not float freely. They are L_i terms whose weights w_i are set by the empirical and historical state space L₀ has already defined. "Attention is all you need" — and the African context sets the attention.

03 · ε · Distributive Justice

The First-Class Residual Signal

Not noise · not error · the desert you came through · ε_observed feeds the update
L_tot = L_0 + Σ w_i·L_i + ε

Distributive Justice is ε — and ε is not residual error. It is the structured absence that the standard model throws away, the signal most visible precisely where data and power are missing. In the standard pipeline, an inequitable outcome is treated as noise — deviation from the mean. In this framework, that inequity is the highest-information token in the system: it tells you exactly where L₀ was mis-specified or where Σw_iL_i failed to constrain action.

Power Dynamics maps ε across LMIC–HIC asymmetries, researcher–participant imbalances, institutional power differentials, and commercialisation risks. Legal & Sovereignty Issues (data ownership, access, intellectual property, national sovereignty) are ε_observed variables — the concrete, measurable manifestation of the residual signal at the legal enforcement boundary.

The Outcome node is where ε_observed triggers the gradient update: streamlined genomic data sharing, a stipulated benefit-sharing framework, and the structural empowerment of every stakeholder and the system itself. An inequitable outcome doesn't mean the principles failed — it means L₀ was specified incorrectly and the system must reconfigure upstream.

ε actively resists ethical imperialism and mandates benefit sharing — not as moral exhortation but as the loss signal that forces the gradient to descend until justice is falsifiably achieved.

04 · L_tot · Core Axiom

The Update Rule

The fractal descent · θ ← θ − η∇L_tot + ε_observed
θ ← θ − η · ∇(Σ w_i L_i) + ε_observed

The framework is not three sequential stages. It is one fractal object with a descent rule. L₀ establishes the prior. Σw_iL_i constrains permissible action via attention-weighted principles. ε is the diagnostic output — the first-class signal that feeds back into L₀ when the system produces inequity.

Each layer is itself expandable: L₀ descends through person → institution → nation → world. Each L_i descends through autonomy's sub-terms — informed consent, re-identifiability, cultural context. ε descends through power differentials → legal gaps → sovereignty claims. This is the fractal: the same update rule applies at every scale.

"If outcomes are inequitable, the error is upstream — in L₀."

Ethics is a function of state space. Systemic capacity is L₀. What is ethically possible is strictly a subset of what L₀ permits. When ε signals inequity, the corrective action is not moral exhortation at the Σw_iL_i level — it is reconfiguration of L₀: the infrastructure, the law, the institutional boundary conditions that make justice achievable.

This is the framework's contribution: not a new set of principles, but a new architecture. GST is not background context. It is the loss function's prior. Principlism is not aspiration. It is the attention-weighted constraint set. Distributive Justice is not the goal stated in a preamble. It is the first-class signal that tells the system whether its gradient descent has converged.
