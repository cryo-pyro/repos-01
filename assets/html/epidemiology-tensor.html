Yearning is not sentiment. It is minus-eta-grad-L — the descent. Structure surfaces that no consultant's report spotted.

Mode 1 — The Extraction Gradient
High-yield districts (Bugisu, Rwenzori, Kasese) load together on value-capture deficit. More coffee produced means more value extracted abroad. Productivity and poverty correlated in the wrong direction.
Mode 2 — The Cultural Non-Market
Domestic absorption near-zero across all clusters. Not one community's failure. A structural absence. Anglican, Catholic, Muslim Uganda alike: the crop was never socialised as a beverage. Not fixable by campaign.
Mode 3 — The Tariff Ceiling
Processing investment clusters near Kampala and Ntungamo but the EU wall creates a hard ceiling. Tariff exposure is upstream of every other processing constraint. AfCFTA is the bypass.
Mode 4 — The GI Premium Signal
Bugisu Arabica already commands a spot premium in specialty markets. GI certification is the fastest price jump requiring no new factories. It is a legal act with a processing-level economic effect.
