The claim is precise: to see is to learn. But the claim requires a corollary — and the corollary is the more uncomfortable proposition: to hear, for most of your musical life, was not learning. Years of composition, organ performance, New Jack Swing–inflected gospel with Heaven Bound, O- and A-level music study — none of it produced harmonic fluency with Bach or Black gospel. The bassline was retrievable. The melody was retrievable. The inner harmonic architecture remained opaque.

Part I · The Pentadic Account of Musical Mastery

I · CALIBRATE The Prior θ

The initial parameter state was genuinely substantial. O- and A-level music training, organist at King's College Budo 1994–1996, six years composing and performing with Heaven Bound. These produced deep, stable encodings of rhythmic structure, melodic contour, and bassline reproduction. The system could track outer voices and groove with genuine fluency.

But in Vaughn Braithwaite's MCR framework — Melody · Chord · Rhythm — the prior was effectively two-dimensional:

The harmonic parameter space was under-specified. Without a coordinate system for the Chord axis, the brain cannot represent extensions (9, 11, 13), altered tones, tritone substitutions, or the inner-voice motion that constitutes advanced harmonic orchestration. Bach and Black gospel appeared as acoustic complexity without coordinates — topology without a map.

II · TASTE The Loss — Sensory Dead-End L(θ)

This is where listening lives. When hearing Bach or advanced gospel harmony, the ear detects a discrepancy between the target and the internal model. Bassline and melody register clearly. The inner harmonic motion collapses into undifferentiated complexity. The system perceives the gap — but cannot resolve it.

"I can hear it but I can't explain what's happening." — the canonical plateau statement, applicable across decades of musical exposure

This is not a failure of effort or of listening hours. It is a structural impossibility. Without a harmonic coordinate system, the loss L(θ) is sensed but not decomposable. The brain experiences the error as diffuse discomfort — "my chords are unconvincing" — but receives no directional signal. The learning rate does not merely slow. It approaches zero, because the gradient is not yet computable.

Listening is sensing, not learning.

II without III → learning rate → ≈ 0

This is the gradient. Before the vocabulary existed, the loss landscape was felt but not navigable. After interval recognition, every chord became a stack of measurable distances. The opaque harmonic field resolved into topology:

The interval drills, harmonic naming exercises, chord-stack analysis, and vocabulary internalization performed exactly one function: they forced the brain to build the harmonic coordinate grid into permanent retrieval. The effort (η) was now being applied along a legible gradient.

V · BODY The Update — Embodied Competence θ′

The internal model updates. Now when hearing Bach or gospel harmony, intervals register immediately, harmonic stacks are inferred, and voice-leading becomes interpretable as geometry rather than noise. The earlier statement becomes true:

Hearing is learning.

Not because hearing changed — because the system can now compute updates from what it hears.

Critically, this is not the end of the loop. It is the beginning of the continuous one. Because θ′ now possesses the vocabulary to compute the gradient, every subsequent musical encounter automatically traverses the full cycle. The plateau disappears.

Part III · Why Bach and Gospel Specifically

Your observation is accurate: pop music allows M+R dominance and the missing Chord dimension does not impede performance. Bach and Black gospel are different because they are harmonic sciences — the entire semantic content lives in the inner voices.

Tradition
Harmonic Signature
Voice-Leading Rule

Every inner voice moves independently by smallest interval
Black Gospel
Heavy extensions · altered dominants · tritone substitutions · chromatic approach

Both traditions solve the same mathematical problem from different coordinates. Bach operates in the language of counterpoint — four independent voices navigating the harmonic field simultaneously. Black gospel operates in the language of jazz-inflected vertical stacks — extended dominants resolving through chromatic gravity. The interval vocabulary functions as a coordinate transform that makes both systems legible from the same perceptual apparatus.

The chord-stack vocabulary you acquired is essentially a coordinate transform that lets the ear perceive harmonic structure that was always present but previously uncomputable.
