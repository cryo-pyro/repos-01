The Mathematical Substrate

One loss function
to rule them all.

Every Ukubona twin formalizes the client's problem as a composite loss over nested stochastic gradient descent. This is not metaphor — it is the operational specification from which all UI, API, and reporting surfaces are derived.

L_total(θ) = Σₖ wₖ Lₖ(θₖ, {θⱼ}j≠k)

where:
L₁ = clinical loss  ·  morbidity, mortality, QoL, toxicity
L₂ = household loss  ·  caregiver burden, access, financial shock
L₃ = system loss  ·  cost efficiency, equity, workforce, supply chain
L₄ = regulatory loss  ·  policy adherence, disease burden, district equity
L₅ = civilizational loss  ·  life expectancy, productivity, sustainability, resilience
