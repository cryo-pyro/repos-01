Step by Step · Do This First

Register Amaani LLC
in Maryland.

This is the legal anchor for everything else. It takes less than an hour and costs $100. Here is exactly what to do.

01
Check Name Availability
Before filing, confirm that "Amaani LLC" is not already taken in Maryland. Use the official entity search tool.
→ Maryland Entity Search
Priority: Amaani LLC → Amaani Systems LLC → Amaani Health LLC
02
Appoint a Resident Agent
Maryland requires a resident agent — a person or registered company with a Maryland street address who can receive legal documents. This can be yourself (if you have a MD address), a friend, or a registered agent service (~$50/year).
Options: Northwest Registered Agent, ZenBusiness, Incfile — all offer MD registered agent service.
03
File Articles of Organization
File online via Maryland Business Express. You'll need: entity name, principal office address, resident agent info, management structure (member-managed), and purpose. Use this exact purpose language:
"To engage in any lawful business for which a limited liability company may be organized in the State of Maryland, including but not limited to: designing and operating data-driven systems for health, education, and social impact; developing and licensing intellectual property; providing consulting and advisory services to public, private, and non-profit organizations; conducting research and implementation of community-based programs, including partnerships with domestic and international organizations; supporting and managing affiliated social impact initiatives, including non-profit entities, in furtherance of mental health, neurodevelopmental, and community well-being. Amaani LLC may conduct business both within and outside the United States."
→ File on Maryland Business Express
Filing fee: $100. Processing: same-day (online).
04
Get Your EIN from the IRS
An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is your company's federal tax ID. You need it to open a bank account, hire, and apply for grants. It's free and takes 10 minutes.
→ Apply for EIN (IRS — Free)
Select: LLC → Sole Member → "Banking Purposes" or "Started a new business."
05
Open a Business Bank Account
Do not mix personal and business funds — especially once grant money flows. You'll need your Articles of Organization + EIN. Mercury Bank (online, free) or a local Maryland credit union both work well for early-stage LLCs.
Mercury Bank is especially good for small LLCs: no minimums, free wires, international transfers supported.
06
File Annual Report (Every Year)
Maryland LLCs must file a Personal Property Return each year (due April 15). The first one isn't due until the year after you register. Set a calendar reminder now.
→ SDAT Annual Filings Portal
Annual fee: $300. Don't miss this — failure to file results in forfeiture of LLC status.
07
Register the Uganda NGO (Next Phase)
Once Amaani LLC is live and has an EIN, you can register Amaani Foundation in Uganda under the NGO Bureau. The Maryland LLC serves as the parent/incorporating body. You will need: certified copy of LLC articles, personal ID, local Uganda address, and a Ugandan board (at least 3 members).
→ Uganda NGO Bureau
Registration fee: ~UGX 100,000. Process takes 4–6 weeks after submission.
