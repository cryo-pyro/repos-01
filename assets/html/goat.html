Why Comparing
States Is Silly.

The Error
θt > θt+1
Ranking Across Time
Asking "who is the GOAT?" treats sequential states as simultaneous competitors. Jordan never played against LeBron. Bar one of Collier's improvisation never competed against bar eight. The comparison strips the loss surface, the era, the gradient — and leaves only a naked number, which means nothing.
The Reframe
θt → θt+1
Tracing the Update
The right question is: what gradient produced this state? Jordan θ1998 is legible only as the output of 14 years of gradient applications — coaches, injuries, rivalries, film. LeBron θ2023 is a different optimization run on a different surface. Both are remarkable. Neither is greater. Both are θ′.
