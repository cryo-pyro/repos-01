$$L_{total} = \underbrace{w_1 L_{thermal} + w_2 L_{evap} + w_3 L_{barrier}}_{\text{set to 0 — cause removed}} + \underbrace{\beta}_{\text{this survives}}$$

The loop doesn't stop. It hallucinates a cause.

05
The Math of Divergence

When β Becomes Its Own Loss Surface.

Here's the formal structure of why phantom-cause loops diverge rather than converge:

Cascade Setup — What fires when cause = ∅
$$L_{phantom} = \beta(\theta) \quad \text{where} \quad \frac{\partial \beta}{\partial \theta} > 0$$

That partial derivative is the key. In a real-cause system, updating θ (scratching, medicating, treating) reduces β because it actually removes the cause. The gradient points downhill toward zero. The loop closes.

In a phantom-cause system, updating θ increases β. Scratching reinforces the C-fiber pathway. Each "correction" validates the system's false belief that a threat exists. The update rule becomes:

The Divergent Update — Phantom Loop
$$\theta' = \theta - \eta \nabla_\theta \beta(\theta) \quad \Rightarrow \quad \beta(\theta') > \beta(\theta)$$

You're descending a gradient that is climbing faster than you descend it. The loss surface is concave up — every step toward the minimum pushes the minimum away. This is why chronic conditions are so resistant: the correction mechanism is itself the amplifier.

In machine learning terms: you have a model that is overfitting to noise, and every training step deepens the overfit rather than resolving it. The solution is not more gradient descent — it's regularization: imposing a penalty on β directly, independent of the loss surface it generates.

Regularized Loss — The Clinical Twin's Real Target
$$L_{reg} = \underbrace{L_{phantom}}_{\text{symptom}} + \lambda \underbrace{\|\beta\|^2}_{\text{penalty on excitability}}$$

The λ term is the clinical intervention: desensitization protocols, neural accommodation, CBT, long-term depression (LTD) of the itch pathway. It is a direct penalty on β — not on the symptoms β generates, but on the bias itself. This is what homeostasis actually means in a phantom-cause regime: not the resolution of an external conflict, but the reduction of internal gain.
